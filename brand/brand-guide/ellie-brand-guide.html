EllieEllıe

Ellie never forgets a scam.

Ellie is a scam-watch agent for older adults. She checks calls, texts and emails, says plainly when something is wrong, and quietly lets family know.

The 10-second pitch

Elephants never forget, and Ellie remembers every scam she's seen. Her big ears listen in on calls and messages. And like the matriarch of a herd, she looks out for the family.

The idea

Three things the mark says without words

An elephant never forgets

Ellie learns every scam pattern reported, from fake parcel fees to "grandson in trouble" calls, and recognises them the next time.

Big ears that listen

She sits in on calls, texts and emails, and notices the tells: urgency, secrecy, gift cards, "don't tell anyone".

She protects the herd

Elephant matriarchs lead and protect their family. Ellie does the same, and quietly lets a trusted family member know when she's stopped something.

Logo

One mark, used four ways

Cream and brass on navy is the main version. On light backgrounds, use the navy badge so the cream elephant keeps its contrast.

Size and space

  • Leave clear space around the mark equal to the width of the trunk on every side.
  • In the lockup, the wordmark's cap height matches the height of the elephant's head.

Please don't

  • Add tusks, a mouth, eyebrows or expressions. Ellie's calm comes from her stillness.
  • Put the mark inside a frame, except the navy app-icon badge.
  • Recolour the ear red or green. Those colours belong to the verdicts.
  • Flip her to face left, stretch her or add shadows.
Colour

Navy for trust, brass for warmth

Option B's palette. Navy and parchment do most of the work, brass adds warmth, and seal red is kept for scam warnings. The logo's cream lives only in the logo.

She speaks in the first person and stays calm. She uses plain words, gives one clear next step, and never makes anyone feel foolish.

Design rules

Built for older eyes and hands

Never colour alone

Every verdict shows an icon, a word and a colour, so it works for colour-blind users too.

Big and easy to tap

Body text is 20px in the app and never below 16px. Buttons are at least 56px tall and full width.

Two choices at most

One main action and one way out. Never a menu of options in a tense moment.

No pressure tactics

No countdowns, flashing or ALL CAPS. Scammers rush people. Ellie never does.

Plain words

Say "fake text", not "phishing attempt". Say "link", not "URL".

Keep the promise visible

"Ellie will never ask for your PIN, password or a code." This line appears on every alert screen.
