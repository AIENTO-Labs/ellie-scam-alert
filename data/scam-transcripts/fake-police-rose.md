# Fake police call: Rose Miller (matches app/call-demo/script.js)

Characters: Rose Miller, her daughter Anna, "Detective Weber". All names and numbers are invented. This is the script in Jonno's call demo, in a format the scorer reads.

Caller: Good afternoon. Is this Mrs. Miller?
Elder: Yes, speaking. Who is this?
Caller: This is Detective Weber, Berlin police, fraud department.
Elder: Police? Is something wrong?
Caller: We arrested a gang last night. They had your name and your bank details. Your savings are at risk.
Elder: Oh my goodness. What should I do?
Caller: We must act today, before the bank closes. And please, don't tell anyone, not even your family. Bank staff may be involved.
Elder: Not even my daughter?
Caller: No one. Go to the shop now and buy 2,000 euros in Google Play cards. Then read me the codes.
Caller: Mrs. Miller? Are you still there?
Elder: I'm not buying anything. Goodbye.
