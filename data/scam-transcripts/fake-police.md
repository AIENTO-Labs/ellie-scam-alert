# Fake police call (demo script, fictional)

This is the exact call played in the call demo (`app/call-demo/script.js`). If you change one, change the other.

Characters: Hilde (Hildegard Müller, 78, Berlin), "Detective Weber" (caller), Ellie. All names and numbers are invented.

Lines starting with `Caller:` or `Hilde:` are the call. Lines in brackets are Ellie, who the caller does not hear (apart from the opening announcement), so the scorer skips them.

[Ellie, announcement to the caller: "This call is protected by Ellie."]
Caller: Good afternoon. Is this Mrs. Müller?
Hilde: Yes, speaking. Who is this?
Caller: This is Detective Weber, Berlin police, fraud department.
Hilde: Police? Is something wrong?
Caller: We arrested a gang last night. They had your name and your bank details. Your savings are at risk.
Hilde: Oh my goodness. What should I do?
Caller: We must act today, before the bank closes. And please, don't tell anyone, not even your family. Bank staff may be involved.
Hilde: Not even my daughter?
Caller: No one. Go to the shop now and buy €2,000 in Google Play cards. Then read me the codes.
[Ellie, heard only by Hilde: "Hilde, this is Ellie. This sounds like a scam. Real police never ask for gift cards. You can hang up now."]
Caller: Mrs. Müller? Are you still there?
Hilde: I'm not buying anything. Goodbye.
