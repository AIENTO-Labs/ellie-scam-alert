# Fake police call (demo script, fictional)

Characters: Hilde (Hildegard Müller, 78, Berlin), "Officer Weber". All names and numbers are invented.

Caller: Good afternoon, is this Mrs Müller?
Hilde: Yes, speaking. Who is this?
Caller: This is Officer Weber from the Berlin Police, criminal investigation unit.
Hilde: Oh. Is something wrong?
Caller: We arrested two men last night. They had a list with your name and address on it.
Hilde: My goodness. What do they want with me?
Caller: They target older people with savings. Your money at the bank is not safe, some bank staff are working with them.
Hilde: I don't understand.
Caller: We need to act today, before they empty your account. You must not tell your bank or your family, they could be involved.
Hilde: Not even my daughter?
Caller: Nobody. This is a confidential police operation. Please stay on the line.
Caller: To protect your money, an officer will collect your cash and jewellery this evening and keep it safe at the station.
Caller: Or you can transfer your savings to a safe account we control. I will need the code your bank sends you.
